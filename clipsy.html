How it works

Three sides, one loop.

01

Campaigns come in

Active clipping campaigns are sourced and aggregated from across the industry — payout rates, platforms, saturation and deadlines normalised into one comparable view.

02

Clippers come in

Traffic from Reddit, X and clipping-adjacent channels lands on clipsy.club and routes into a managed Discord community where the actual coordination happens.

03

Distribution goes out

Coordinated clippers push campaign footage into native posts across TikTok, Instagram, YouTube and X — with performance tracked per campaign.

Why we built it

We’d rather run campaigns than take a cut of someone else’s.

Clipping is one of the most efficient distribution mechanisms available to a brand right now, and it’s almost completely unorganised. Campaigns are scattered across platforms, discovery is word-of-mouth, and most operators sit downstream taking a percentage.

Apex Media built Clipsy to sit at the front of that funnel instead: own the discovery layer, own the community, and run campaigns directly for the brands that bring them to us.

Aggregation layer

Campaigns from across the clipping industry, normalised into one browsable feed.

Managed community

A Discord operation where clippers get briefed, coordinated and supported.

Growth channels

Reddit, X and clipping-adjacent channels feeding qualified traffic into the funnel.

Direct brand deals

Brands bringing campaigns straight to Apex Media, run through the Clipsy network.
