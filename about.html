About

A media company
built in Puerto Rico.

Apex Media LLC is a member-managed media company operating out of Dorado, Puerto Rico. We run content operations for creators and brands, and we own and operate Clipsy, our clipping-campaign platform.

What we do

Media production, creator management, and distribution.

Our chartered purpose is straightforward: media production, talent and creator management, social media and community management — including Discord community operations and content clipping services — influencer and brand marketing campaigns, and media and sponsorship deal negotiation.

In practice that means we function as an outsourced media department. We film, we edit, we publish, we manage the community, and we handle the commercial layer on top. Clients get one team accountable for the whole pipeline instead of four freelancers and a group chat.

We are deliberately small and hands-on. Every member of the company works on delivery.

Structure

One parent company,
one owned platform.

Apex Media LLC is the operating entity. Clipsy (clipsy.club) is a brand and platform wholly owned and operated by the company — not a separate entity, not a joint venture.
