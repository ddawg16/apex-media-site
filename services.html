Social Media Management

We run the accounts. Strategy, calendar, publishing, and the comment section — treated as one operating system rather than six disconnected apps.

  • Platform strategy and positioning per channel
  • Daily publishing across Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and Snapchat
  • Caption, hook and hashtag writing
  • Comment-section and DM community management
  • Trend monitoring and rapid-response posting
  • Weekly performance reporting with what we’re changing next

Video Editing & Post-Production

High-volume short-form editing built for retention: cold-open hooks, burned-in captions, motion graphics, and sound design that survives a muted first second.

  • Short-form edits for Reels, TikTok, Shorts and Snapchat
  • Hook-first structure with retention-driven pacing
  • Burned-in karaoke captions and kinetic type
  • Motion graphics, charts and data animations
  • Voiceover-first workflow so visuals sync to speaker pacing
  • Batch delivery on a daily or weekly cadence

Content Production & Recording

We come to the shoot. On-set and on-location capture with broadcast-grade audio, so the raw material is worth editing in the first place.

  • On-set, on-location and studio capture
  • Sony mirrorless bodies, multicam coverage
  • SM7B + Rodecaster Pro studio audio chain
  • DJI wireless rigs for man-on-the-street and run-and-gun

Clipping & Distribution

One asset, hundreds of native posts. We operate multi-account clipping networks with content-ratio strategy so distribution keeps working after the first wave.

  • Campaign sourcing and clipper coordination via Clipsy
  • Verticals currently operated: finance, minerals & resources, AI & tech
  • Performance tracking per campaign and per account

How an engagement runs

Four stages, repeated weekly.

01

Intake & strategy

We audit what exists, define the formats, set the cadence, and agree on what success looks like in numbers.

02

Production

We film, we source, we script. Raw material gets captured properly so the edit isn’t a rescue mission.

03

Post & distribution

Editing, captioning, scheduling, publishing, and clipping into every channel the content should reach.

04

Report & adjust

Performance goes back into the next batch. What worked gets scaled, what didn’t gets cut.
