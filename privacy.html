Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

This policy explains how Apex Media LLC (“Apex Media”, “we”, “us”) handles information collected through apexmedia.space. We keep this deliberately short because we collect very little.

Information we collect

This website does not use analytics cookies, advertising trackers, or third-party tracking pixels. The only personal information we receive is what you choose to send us: the name, email address, company or handle, and message you submit through the contact form, which opens in your own email client and is sent directly to us by email.

How we use it

We use the information you send solely to respond to your enquiry, provide services you request, and maintain ordinary business records. We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Third-party services

This site is hosted by Vercel Inc., which processes standard server request data (such as IP address and browser user-agent) for the purpose of serving the website and protecting it from abuse. Fonts are served by Google Fonts. Email sent to us is processed by our email provider. Each of these providers operates under its own privacy policy.

Retention

We keep enquiry correspondence for as long as needed to serve the relationship and to meet legal, accounting and tax obligations, then delete it.

Your rights

You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to hello@apexmedia.space and we will respond within a reasonable period.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
